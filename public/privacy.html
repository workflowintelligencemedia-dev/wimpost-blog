Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026

At Wimpost (Workflow Intelligence Media), accessible from https://wimpost.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by Wimpost and how we use it.

Log Files and Analytics

Wimpost follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

Cookies and Google AdSense DoubleClick DART Cookie

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to wimpost.com and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Third-Party Privacy Policies

Wimpost's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. We advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information about their practices and instructions on how to opt out of certain options.

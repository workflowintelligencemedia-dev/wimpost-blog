How to Fix Mispronounced Brand Names & Acronyms in ElevenLabs Dubbing Studio

Published: October 15, 2026 • Technical Troubleshooting Guide • 4 min read
The 30-Second Quick Answer: To fix mispronounced brand names, creator channels, or technical acronyms in ElevenLabs Dubbing Studio, create a .pls (Pronunciation Lexicon Specification) XML file using <grapheme> for the written word and <alias> for the phonetic sound, upload it under Project Settings › Pronunciation Dictionaries, and click Re-synthesize on the affected audio track.

Why Hitting "Regenerate" Doesn't Solve the Problem

When an AI voice mispronounces a unique creator handle or acronym (like pronouncing "Wimpost" with an awkward silent letter or spelling out words that should be pronounced as a single syllable), many creators repeatedly hit "Regenerate." This wastes monthly audio synthesis credits without fixing the underlying phonetic model. Instead, you need to provide an explicit pronunciation rule.

Step 1: Copy This Working `.pls` Dictionary Template

Create a plain text file on your computer named brand-dictionary.pls and paste the following XML template:

<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
<lexicon version="1.0" 
      xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2005/01/pronunciation-lexicon"
      alphabet="ipa" xml:lang="en-US">
  <lexeme>
    <grapheme>Wimpost</grapheme>
    <alias>Wim-post</alias>
  </lexeme>
  <lexeme>
    <grapheme>API</grapheme>
    <alias>A P I</alias>
  </lexeme>
</lexicon>

Step 2: Choose the Right Method: Alias vs. Phoneme

Depending on whether you are correcting an acronym, a brand name, or an accent, choose between an <alias> tag or an International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) <phoneme> tag:

Tag Method Best Used For Example Syntax Difficulty
Alias Tag (<alias>) Brand names, acronyms, and phonetic respelling in plain English. <alias>Descript</alias> Beginner (Recommended)
Phoneme Tag (<phoneme>) Strict vowel control, foreign language terms, and specific regional dialects. <phoneme>ˈtəmɑːtəʊ</phoneme> Advanced (Requires IPA)

Step 3: Attach the Dictionary in Dubbing Studio

  1. Open your project inside ElevenLabs Dubbing Studio.
  2. Open the project settings panel on the right sidebar and navigate to Pronunciation Dictionaries.
  3. Upload your brand-dictionary.pls file.
  4. Select the speaker track containing the mispronounced word and click Re-synthesize Track. Only that segment will update, preserving the rest of your dubbed timeline.

Summary Checklist

  • Ensure your file extension is strictly .pls, not .pls.txt.
  • Keep your grapheme matching exact casing (e.g., matching capitalized acronyms).
  • Always test audio on a single short sentence before re-exporting your full multi-language timeline.
